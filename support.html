How the app works

  • Pendulum, Answer Book, and Tarot are rituals for reflection and entertainment. A reading is not medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice.
  • Pendulum: gently shake your iPhone. It answers yes or no. Shake needs a real device, not the Simulator.
  • Answer Book: open the book. One page gives a short reply.
  • Tarot: one card for the local day. Opening it does not use a free reading.
  • Pendulum and Answer Book share 10 free readings. Reset Everything does not restore readings you have already used.
  • After that, lifetime access is a one-time purchase for Pendulum and Answer Book. It is not a subscription and it does not renew. Tarot stays available without that purchase.
  • If you add a reading to My Journal, what you type stays on this device. It is not sent to us. An Answer Book entry can be edited or deleted from its page.
  • Choose Restore Purchases in Settings to return lifetime access on a device with the same Apple ID.

Purchases and refunds

Purchases are billed by Apple. A refund for lifetime access is requested from Apple, not from the developer.
