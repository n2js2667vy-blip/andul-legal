How the app works

  • Hold a question in mind. The app does not ask you to type it, and it is not saved.
  • Pendulum: gently shake your iPhone. It answers yes or no. Shake needs a real device, not the Simulator.
  • Answer Book: open the book. One page gives a short reply.
  • You can use Andül 10 times at no charge. Pendulum and Answer Book share those 10 readings.
  • After that, lifetime access is a one-time purchase. It is not a subscription and it does not renew.
  • Choose Restore Purchases in Settings to return lifetime access on a device with the same Apple ID.
  • A reading is for reflection and entertainment. It is not medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice.

Purchases and refunds

Purchases are billed by Apple. A refund for lifetime access is requested from Apple, not from the developer.
