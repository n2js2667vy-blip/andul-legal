Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Andül is offered by Alper Halıcı. This policy describes what the app handles and what it does not.

Your question stays with you

Pendulum and Answer Book ask you to hold a question in mind. The app does not provide a place to type that question, and it is not saved or sent to us or to anyone else.

Stored only on your device

Andül keeps a few preferences on the device itself: the direction your pendulum uses for Yes, and whether haptic feedback is on. It will also keep, on the device, how many of the 10 free readings you have used. This information is not uploaded to a server we operate. We do not run an account system.

Purchases

Lifetime access is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. Apple receives the payment details associated with your Apple ID. We do not receive your card number, billing address, or Apple ID password. The app receives only what it needs to unlock lifetime access and to restore that purchase on your devices. Restore Purchases asks Apple whether this Apple ID already bought lifetime access.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or advertising identifier. Andül does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share personal information. We do not use your information to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Apple diagnostics

If you have allowed Apple to share analytics with developers, Apple may send us crash information under Apple’s own settings. That sharing is controlled by your device, not by an Andül account. It does not include the question you had in mind.

Children

Andül is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

How long information remains

Preferences and the free-reading count remain on the device until you delete the app or remove its data. Apple keeps the lifetime purchase record under Apple’s policies, so Restore Purchases can return access on a device signed in with the same Apple ID.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in Settings and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to alperhalici.85@outlook.com, or through Contact Support in Settings. You can also use the Support page.
